How to read this page

  • Cash basis, calendar year. A row is recorded on the date money actually moves, not when a bill arrives or a gift is promised. The year runs January 1 to December 31.
  • Amounts. Money in is positive. Money out has a minus sign. Balance is the bank balance after that row.
  • Paid directly by donor. Sometimes a donor pays one of our bills with their own money and does not want it back. That appears as two rows on the same date: the gift and the matching expense. No money passes through the bank, so the balance does not move.
  • Receipts and statements. Every expense links to its receipt, and every closed month links to its bank statement. Before publishing we remove card numbers and account numbers except the last four digits, routing numbers, personal addresses and phone numbers, and donor names. Nothing else is changed.
  • Donor names. Donors are listed as Individual donor, Foundation, Church or Government unless a donor asks to be named.
  • Salaries. No one is paid, so there is no salary category and the figure is always $0.00.
  • Share spent on programs. Program spending divided by all spending for the year. The first year reads low because the fees to form the organization are its only costs so far.
  • Corrections. Once a month is closed, its rows are never edited or deleted. A mistake is fixed in the open with a row that reverses it and a new, correct row.
