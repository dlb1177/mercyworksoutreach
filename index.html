Mercy Works Outreach is a Christian street outreach ministry. We bring clothing, blankets, hygiene items and food to people in need in Minneapolis and the surrounding area, free of charge and one person at a time.

New and still forming. Mercy Works Outreach was incorporated as a Minnesota nonprofit corporation on November 18, 2026. The steps still ahead are listed below, and this site will fill in as each one is finished.

What we do

We go to people where they are and give them what they need that day: clothing, blankets, hygiene items, food and other necessities. Everything is given freely.

As an expression of Christian faith, volunteers also offer prayer, Bibles and devotional materials to anyone who wishes to receive them.

What we hold to

  1. Radical transparency

    We publish our formation documents, our board minutes and every transaction, down to the penny. That is far more than the law asks of a nonprofit. Anyone who supports this work should be able to see exactly where the money goes.

  2. 100% volunteer-run

    No one is paid, and no one ever will be. The limit keeps the ministry small and personal, close to the example of the Missionaries of Charity.

  3. Highly efficient

    We spend nothing on fundraising, advertising or administration beyond the minimum fees the law requires. We choose simple systems over complicated ones.

  4. Focused on the core mission

    We do what our articles of incorporation describe and nothing more. There is more than enough need in Minneapolis to keep us at it.

  5. Fearless without being careless

    We try to meet each person the way Jesus would: at their level, close up, with dignity, like a member of our own family. We don't let fear keep us from serving, and we stay aware of our surroundings and put safety first.
