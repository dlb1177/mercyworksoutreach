Mercy Works Outreach is an independent, volunteer-run ministry rooted in the Catholic faith, serving people experiencing homelessness in the Twin Cities. We provide food, clothing, blankets, and hygiene items, free of charge, to one person at a time.

What we do

We meet people where they are and give them what they need that day.

We are not seeking to solve homelessness. We simply dignify each person by name, providing a momentary break from hunger, thirst and cold, and from despair, invisibility and hopelessness.

As an expression of Catholic faith, volunteers also offer prayer, Bibles and devotional materials to anyone who wishes to receive them.

What we hold to

  1. Fearless without being careless

    Our Catholic faith calls us to meet each person the way Jesus would: one at a time, at their level, close enough to take their hand. We treat everyone we serve as a member of our own family, with all the dignity that that implies.

  2. Focused on the core mission

    We do only what our publicly available articles of incorporation describe as our founding purpose and nothing more. There is more than enough need in the Twin Cities to keep us focused on what we set out to do from the start.

  3. Highly efficient

    We spend nothing on fundraising or advertising, and nothing on administration beyond the essentials, such as the fees the law requires, insurance and the small cost of this website. We choose simple systems over complicated ones to keep our resources going where they are needed most.

  4. 100% volunteer-run

    No one is paid, and no one ever will be. The limit keeps the ministry small and personal, close to the example of the Missionaries of Charity.

  5. Radical transparency

    We publish our formation documents, our board minutes and every transaction, down to the penny. That is far more than the law asks of a nonprofit. Anyone who supports this work should be able to see exactly where the money goes.

Inspiration

Our mark is a cup with a single drop of water above it. It comes from two sayings of Jesus: “I thirst” (John 19:28) and “I was thirsty and you gave me drink” (Matthew 25:35). Every coat, blanket and meal we hand out is that cup of water, given person to person.

Mercy Works Outreach is not affiliated with the Missionaries of Charity, founded by St. Teresa of Calcutta, but we draw great inspiration from their work to serve the poorest of the poor in person, with few resources and nothing wasted. This is one of her prayers:

Mary, our dearest Mother,
give us your heart
so beautiful, so pure,
so immaculate,
your heart so full of love and humility
that we may be able to receive
Jesus in the Bread of Life,
love Him as you loved Him,
and serve Him
in the distressing disguise
of the poorest of the poor.
Amen.

St. Teresa of Calcutta
