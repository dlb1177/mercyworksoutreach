Mercy Works Outreach is an outreach ministry. We bring clothing, blankets, hygiene items and food to people in need in Minneapolis and the surrounding area, free of charge and one person at a time.

New and still forming. Mercy Works Outreach was incorporated as a Minnesota nonprofit corporation on November 18, 2026. This site will fill in as the organization does.

What we do

We go to people where they are and give them what they need that day: clothing, blankets, hygiene items, food and other necessities. Everything is given freely.

As an expression of Catholic faith, volunteers also offer prayer, Bibles and devotional materials to anyone who wishes to receive them.

What we hold to

  1. Fearless without being careless

    Our Catholic faith calls us to meet each person the way Jesus would: at their level, close up, with dignity, like a member of our own family. We don't let fear keep us from serving, and we stay aware of our surroundings and put safety first.

  2. Focused on the core mission

    We do what our articles of incorporation describe and nothing more. There is more than enough need in Minneapolis to keep us at it.

  3. Highly efficient

    We spend nothing on fundraising, advertising or administration beyond the minimum fees the law requires. We choose simple systems over complicated ones.

  4. 100% volunteer-run

    No one is paid, and no one ever will be. The limit keeps the ministry small and personal, close to the example of the Missionaries of Charity.

  5. Radical transparency

    We publish our formation documents, our board minutes and every transaction, down to the penny. That is far more than the law asks of a nonprofit. Anyone who supports this work should be able to see exactly where the money goes.

Inspiration

Our mark is a cup with a single drop of water above it. It comes from two sayings of Jesus: “I thirst” (John 19:28) and “I was thirsty and you gave me drink” (Matthew 25:35). Every coat, blanket and meal we hand out is that cup of water, given person to person.

We look to the Missionaries of Charity for how to serve: in person, with few resources and nothing wasted. We are not affiliated with them.
