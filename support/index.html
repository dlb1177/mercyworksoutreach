Mercy Works Outreach is run entirely by volunteers. We pay no salaries and spend nothing on fundraising or advertising. Gifts pay for the food, clothing, blankets and hygiene items we hand out, and for the few essentials of running the ministry, such as the fees the law requires, insurance and the small cost of this website.
