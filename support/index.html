Support

Mercy Works Outreach is run entirely by volunteers. We pay no salaries and spend nothing on fundraising or advertising. We take gifts online through Zeffy, a donation platform that charges us no fees, so all of your gift reaches us. There are two campaigns you can give to. Choose one to open it.

Give 1,000 Winter Care Kits

$5.85 puts a winter care kit in a neighbor’s hands. This winter our goal is to hand out 1,000 kits, one person at a time, to neighbors living on the street. Gifts to this campaign buy winter care kits.

The first 100 are packed and in our inventory. What they cost is listed under Donated Goods.

Open this campaign on Zeffy

Help Launch Mercy Works Outreach

Your gift helps us launch. Gifts to this campaign pay for the food, clothing, blankets and hygiene items we hand out, and for the few essentials of running the ministry, such as the fees the law requires, insurance and the small cost of this website.

Before You Give

  • Zeffy may suggest a tip. At checkout, Zeffy suggests an optional contribution to Zeffy itself. It does not come to us, and you can change it to $0.
  • Tax status. We have applied to the IRS for recognition as a tax-exempt charity and are waiting for its decision. Until the IRS decides, we cannot tell you that a gift is tax-deductible. Our filings are on the Tax Documents page.
  • Your gift is public; your name is not. Every gift appears in our public ledger by date and amount. A donor is named only if they ask to be.
  • Questions? Write to info@mercyworksoutreach.org.

Other Ways to Help

  • Volunteer. Join us on an outreach night. Write to info@mercyworksoutreach.org.
  • Share. Send this page to someone who would care.
  • Pray. For the people we meet and for the volunteers who serve them.
  • Give goods. A list of the items we need is coming soon.
