Support

Mercy Works Outreach is run entirely by volunteers. We pay no salaries and spend nothing on fundraising or advertising. Gifts pay for the food, clothing, blankets and hygiene items we hand out, and for the few essentials of running the ministry, such as the fees the law requires, insurance and the small cost of this website.

Give Online

We take gifts online through Zeffy, a donation platform that charges us no fees, so all of your gift reaches us.

Give on our Zeffy page

Or give here, without leaving this site:

  • Zeffy may suggest a tip. At checkout, Zeffy suggests an optional contribution to Zeffy itself. It does not come to us, and you can change it to $0.
  • Tax status. We have applied to the IRS for recognition as a tax-exempt charity and are waiting for its decision. Until the IRS decides, we cannot tell you that a gift is tax-deductible. Our filings are on the Tax Documents page.
  • Your gift is public; your name is not. Every gift appears in our public ledger by date and amount. A donor is named only if they ask to be.
  • Questions? Write to info@mercyworksoutreach.org.
